# AI Generator UX Observation

### UX-OBS-01
**Title:** Handling of unsupported characters in role/format fields

**Precondition:** User is on AI Resume Generator page

**Steps:**
1. Enter emojis or unusual symbols (e.g., ✅, 😎) in role or format fields
2. Click **Згенерувати**

**Observed Behavior:** AI generates a prompt successfully without errors

**Note / Comment:** Output is very general and does not reflect the specific symbols used; the system does not crash, but interpretation is fuzzy

**Severity:** Low

**Priority:** Medium
